The Verdant Veins are invisible energy currents running beneath the [[The Great Swamp|Great Swamp]] and the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], binding water, root, soil, and living things into a single interconnected network. They are not understood the same way by any two groups who know of them, and the gap between those understandings has shaped the history of the region as much as anything visible or measurable.

## Description
---
The Veins flow beneath the swamp in branching currents that follow water, root systems, and certain mineral deposits in the peat. They are not visible to ordinary perception but are felt by those attuned to them as a kind of ambient vitality, a quality of aliveness in the ground and water that is difficult to describe to someone who has not experienced it. The swamp is healthier, stranger, and more alive where the Veins run strongly. Where they thin or are disrupted, the land reflects it.

[[Mother Tree|Vethara]], the ancient thornspire mother tree at the center of the Thornspire Heartwood, is the anchor point of the entire network. Her roots extend through the heartwood plateau and outward into the swamp, and the Veins follow them, drawing their strength from her presence. The extension trees throughout the Heartwood are physical offshoots of her root network and cannot survive without it. When the Veins are healthy, their influence extends outward from the Heartwood in a way that brightens the surrounding swamp, intensifying its characteristic qualities of growth, decay, and strange life. When they are diminished, that influence contracts back toward the protected core.

## Cultural Interpretation
---
The [[Swampfolk]] regard the Verdant Veins as a sacred trust, something to be tended rather than controlled or extracted from. Their understanding of the Veins is intuitive and communal rather than systematic, passed through generations of living alongside them rather than through formal study. The traditional practice of limited [[Mireblood]] extraction reflects this understanding: taking sparingly from what the Veins produce is acceptable, taking without care is not.

The [[Thornkin]] understand the Veins more directly, through their relationship with Vethara and through the [[Mossbough|Mossbough bond]] that connects their leader to the mother tree. Their stewardship of the Heartwood is inseparable from their stewardship of the Veins, and the health of one is the health of the other. The Closing of the Heartwood following the [[Witch's Sundering|Witch's Sundering]] was partly Vethara's response to the threat posed to the Veins by uncontrolled Mireblood extraction.

The witches who were exiled following the Sundering had a different relationship to the Veins, one oriented toward manipulation and amplification through brewing and ritual rather than stewardship. It was the consequences of this approach, specifically the way Morwenna's example drew others with less careful intentions, that precipitated the Sundering itself.

[[Morwenna of the Hollow]] now tends a cutting of Vethara's lineage in the northern mangrove swamp. That cutting has begun producing nascent Veins of its own in the north, a development whose significance is not yet understood by anyone aware of it.

## The Cauldron Tap
---
The [[Cauldron Tap]], an industrial Mireblood extraction structure built by the [[Sap Suckers]] at a Verdant Veins intersection west of [[Reedmarsh]], is slowly draining the network. The extraction is not targeted at the Veins directly but at the Mireblood they produce, yet the effect on the broader network is cumulative and ongoing. The Veins have been weakening since the Tap's installation, a process that manifests most visibly in the declining health of the current [[Mossbough]], whose bond with Vethara means she experiences the Veins' diminishment as a physical deterioration. The cause of this decline has not been identified by anyone currently in a position to act on it.

The Sap Suckers, operating as outside agents with no knowledge of or investment in the Veins' significance, are indifferent to the damage they are causing. The residual post-processed material that seeps back into the surrounding peat from the Tap's operation adds a secondary layer of disruption whose effects are separate from the extraction itself.

## Current State
---
The Verdant Veins are under sustained pressure from the Cauldron Tap's operation and have been contracting toward the Heartwood's protected core for some time. The moorland at the former outer boundary of the Heartwood, where the extension trees died back following the Witch's Sundering, marks the outer limit of what the Veins currently sustain. Beyond it, the swamp is noticeably different in character, less intensely alive, though whether ordinary inhabitants of Reedmarsh would articulate it in those terms is another question.

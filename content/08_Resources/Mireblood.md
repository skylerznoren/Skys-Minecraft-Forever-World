[[Mireblood]] is the alchemical substance that forms deep beneath the peat of [[The Great Swamp|the Great Swamp]], where centuries of organic decay, mineral pressure, and magical residue concentrate into a dense, slow-moving liquid. It is the physical expression of the [[Verdant Veins]] made tangible, and is considered sacred by every people who have lived in the swamp long enough to understand what it is. It is known colloquially as [[Mireblood|Witchsap.]], a name that originated with outside traders but has since become common currency within the swamp as well.
## Appearance
---
Mireblood is a sludgy, peat-like material that catches light in a way that seems at odds with its dark origins. Its base is a deep, near-black purple, shot through with a shimmer of green that surfaces depending on the angle of the light. Those who encounter it for the first time often remark that it looks as though it is still alive.

## Origins
---
Mireblood accumulates over centuries beneath compressed layers of peat and root. The decay of plants, animals, and minerals combines with the latent magical current of the Verdant Veins to produce a viscous essence that behaves as though it belongs to something living. It does not form quickly. What the [[Cauldron Tap]] extracts in a season may have taken generations to accumulate.

## Properties
---
Mireblood can be refined into several distinct alchemical yields, each corresponding to a different component of its composition.

- **Redstone** -- crystallizes from volatile compounds that condense under long periods of magical pressure. Once used by practitioners to power ritual arrays and extend potion potency.
- **Gunpowder** -- precipitates from an unstable mineral compound within the liquid, rich in naturally occurring saltpeter, charcoal, and sulfur.
- **Glowstone dust** -- derived from bioluminescent fungal spores suspended within especially potent batches. Their golden shimmer is a reliable indicator of quality.
- **Spider eyes** -- the preserved remains of swamp predators buried and saturated over centuries, valued for their toxic alchemical properties.
- **Sugar** -- formed from crystallized reed resins that seep into the peat and absorb magical charge over time.
- **Glass bottles** -- surface intact from the deep peat, remnants of old witch workings and ritual sites the swamp swallowed long ago, preserved by the Mireblood around them.
- **Sticks** -- ancient wood, petrified and preserved in the mud by centuries of peat compression.

## Cultural Significance
---
To [[The Swampfolk of Reedmarsh|the Swampfolk of Reedmarsh]] and the people of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], Mireblood is sacred. If it is not taken sparingly, then it must not be taken at all. Overharvesting is understood not merely as ecological damage but as a spiritual trespass against the living body of the swamp. [[Grandmother Eswa]] of Reedmarsh, among the eldest living inhabitants of the swamp, is one of several elders who remember what the swamp felt like before extraction began at its current scale.

To the [[Exiled Witches]], Mireblood was and remains a birthright, a catalyst for their craft. Their growing hunger to accelerate its harvest and bend the Verdant Veins to their will was among the central causes of the [[Witch's Sundering]].

To outside parties, it is a commodity.

## Extraction
---
The Cauldron Tap extracts Mireblood in volumes no prior method approached. What the swamp's inhabitants call the [[Sap Suckers]] move the product out of the swamp in unmarked barrels by cart, passing themselves off as ordinary traders once on the road. Where it goes from there is not known to anyone in the swamp.

The [[Pact of Root & Reed]] is a formal agreement between the people of [[Reedmarsh]] and the people of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], establishing the terms of mutual cooperation between the two communities. It is among the oldest documented agreements in the region and remains the foundation of the relationship between the two settlements to this day.

## Background
---
The pact was drafted by [[Taarvin Mossbough]], then leader of the Thornspire Heartwood, and signed alongside Head Elder [[Fensar Reedhand]] of Reedmarsh, tenth great-grandfather of the current head elder. The two communities had long been allies by disposition and proximity, but the growing instability surrounding what would become the [[Witch's Sundering]] made that goodwill feel insufficient. The Pact was not the beginning of their alliance. It was the decision to anchor it in writing while there was still time to do so calmly, before the situation demanded it.

## Terms
---
The pact addressed free passage between the two settlements, shared stewardship of the waterways running between them, and a mutual defense obligation requiring each party to warn the other of outside threats. The Heartwood pledged shelter and resource sharing in times of need. Reedmarsh pledged vigilance over the waters and approaches to the south.

## The Text
---
> *By root and by reed, let this be witnessed.*
>
> *We of the Heartwood hold what is old and deep. We of Reedmarsh hold what is patient and enduring. Neither is whole without the other, and neither shall act as though otherwise.*
>
> *Free passage between our peoples. Fair sharing of what the land provides. Warning given before harm can reach what we both protect.*
>
> *Signed under the Great Bough, by Taarvin Mossbough and Head Elder Fensar Reedhand of Reedmarsh.*

## Legacy
---
The Pact has held without formal renegotiation since its signing. Its terms are not written into daily life so much as assumed by it. The violence that both communities feared at the time of signing never fully materialized, but the Pact remained. The arrival of the [[Cauldron Tap]] within living memory represents the first outside threat of the scale the document was written to address.

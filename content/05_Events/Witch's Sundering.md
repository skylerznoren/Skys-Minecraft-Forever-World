The Witch's Sundering was the fracture between the communities of [[Reedmarsh]] and the [[Thornspire Heartwood]] and those among them who would become known as the [[Exiled Witches]], ending in the exile of [[Morwenna of the Hollow]] and her followers into the northern reaches of [[The Great Swamp|the Great Swamp]]. It remains the defining event of the region, and its consequences have never fully resolved.

## Causes
---
The conflict grew from a disagreement about the proper relationship between magical practitioners and the [[Verdant Veins]]. The traditions of both Reedmarsh and the Heartwood held that [[Mireblood]] must be taken sparingly and always in balance with what the land could sustain. Morwenna, then a respected healer and seer of Reedmarsh, had been extracting beyond what tradition permitted, using it to fuel magical and alchemical work she believed justified the cost. Her personal extractions were, by most reasonable measure, too small to damage the swamp in any lasting way. She knew this, and said so plainly.

What the elders of Reedmarsh and the Heartwood had also seen, and what Morwenna had not fully reckoned with, was the effect of her example on others. Practitioners with less careful intentions had begun extracting in her name and with her arguments, and the harm the elders had long feared was beginning to materialize; not through Morwenna's own hands, but through those who had gathered around her. When they moved against her, they were moving against everything her example had set in motion, not only what she had done herself. Morwenna understood this distinction. The elders did not accept it as sufficient.

The Heartwood's position was sharpened by something more personal. Morwenna had been trained by the Thornkin, given access to the interior of the Heartwood, taught the pruning traditions that no outsider was meant to know, and brought into the hollow of [[Mother Tree|Vethara]] herself as part of that training. The investment the Thornkin had made in her was without precedent. [[Taarvin Mossbough]] felt her actions as a particular kind of betrayal, one that the Heartwood would carry long after the confrontation was over.

## The Confrontation
---
The final meeting was held at the base of the mother tree in the Thornspire Heartwood, at the great root table where the communities had long gathered to discuss matters of shared concern. Morwenna met with Head Elder [[Fensar Reedhand]] of Reedmarsh and Taarvin Mossbough of the Heartwood, her followers present behind her. The discussion did not remain civil for long. Many of those who had aligned themselves with Morwenna were not there to negotiate, and their anger made it impossible for her to be heard on her own terms. She could not separate herself from what they represented without abandoning them entirely, and she would not do that.

At some point during the confrontation, in frustration at her own inability to make herself understood, Morwenna broke a branch from the root table. The table went quiet. Whether she meant anything by it or not, the gesture was read as a declaration. The meeting had ended. The Heartwood guards followed the group all the way to the edge of the forest and did not stop walking until Morwenna and her followers had passed out of sight into the swamp.

She carried the broken branch with her.

## Aftermath
---
Morwenna led her followers north into the wilder reaches of the swamp, beyond the stabilizing reach of the Heartwood's roots, where the Verdant Veins run thick and unregulated. The southeastern communities have had no formal contact with them since. In the immediate aftermath, the Thornspire Heartwood and Reedmarsh formalized their existing alliance through the [[Pact of Root & Reed]], anchoring themselves together against a situation both communities feared could yet turn violent. It did not, but the Pact remained.

What the communities of the southeastern swamp did not fully understand at the time was what Taarvin's grief passed into Vethara through the bond between them. She responded as she responds to all things, not with intention but with the complete and unhesitating reaction of something immeasurably old. The full strength of the Verdant Veins pulled back toward the protected core of the Heartwood. The thorned roots sealed every outer approach except the southern passage through Reedmarsh. The outer zone of the Heartwood, its markets and open paths and the world the Thornkin had built facing outward, was cut off from the root network and died back over the generations that followed, leaving the moorland that stands there now. This is not how the Thornkin remember it, or discuss it, if they discuss it at all.

Among the Swampfolk, Morwenna is remembered as the one whose ambition drew others into exploitation and whose pride made reconciliation impossible. The fear that she or her followers may one day return has never entirely left the region. Among those who followed her, the telling is different.

Morwenna herself has not been seen in generations. Most assume she died long ago. A few older residents of Reedmarsh are less certain, though they would struggle to say why.

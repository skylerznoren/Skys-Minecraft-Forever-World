This wiki is built in Obsidian and hosted publicly through Quartz. It is designed to be explored rather than read linearly, the way a world is explored rather than summarized. This article covers everything you need to navigate it comfortably.

## The Layout
---
The site has three main areas. On the left is the folder navigator, which organizes every article by type. Use it to browse broadly or find something specific. On the right is the graph view, a visual map of how everything connects to everything else. In the center is the article itself. Clicking any link in an article takes you directly to that article. Clicking one of the article's tags shows you a list of all other articles with the same tag. Clicking the site header in the top left returns you to the home page from anywhere.

A search bar sits above the folder navigator. It searches across all article titles and content simultaneously and is the fastest way to find something specific.

## The Folder Structure
---
Articles are organized into the following folders in the navigator:

**00 Meta** is where you are now. It contains articles about the project itself rather than the world, including this one, the ethical statement, and other behind the scenes documentation.

**01 Places** covers every named location in the world, from major regions down to individual buildings and landmarks.

**02 People** covers named individuals, historical and living, significant to the world's history and present.

**03 Groups** covers factions, cultures, organizations, and peoples.

**04 Culture** covers belief systems, traditions, and social practices that shape how communities in the world operate.

**05 Events** covers significant historical moments that have shaped the present state of the world.

**06 Concepts** covers ideas, systems, and phenomena that do not fit neatly into any other category, magical systems, recurring motifs, and the like.

**07 Items** covers notable objects with their own histories and significance.

**08 Resources** covers materials and substances that play a meaningful role in the world's economy or lore.

## Reading Articles
---
Articles are written from an outside perspective, as though documenting a real place, rather than as game guides or build logs. Each article has a properties block at the top showing its type, region, status, tags, dates, and an estimated reading time.

A status of draft means the article is incomplete or subject to change. Most articles are currently drafts. ==Text highlighted like this== marks something unresolved or still being figured out. I keep those highlights visible rather than hiding them because the gaps are part of the honest picture of where the project stands.

Links within articles connect to related articles elsewhere in the wiki. Following them is the best way to understand how the world fits together. The graph view on the right makes those connections visible all at once if you prefer a broader picture before diving in.

## A Note On Depth
---
Not everything is explained. Some things are left deliberately vague, some histories are only partially known even within the world's fiction, and some articles are stubs that will grow over time. This is intentional. I am building a world that feels old and layered, the kind of place where not everything has a clean answer. If something seems unresolved, it probably is, and that is a feature rather than an oversight.

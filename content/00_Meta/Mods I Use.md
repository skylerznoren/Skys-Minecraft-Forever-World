This article lists every mod, data pack, and texture pack I run on this world. I think being upfront about this stuff matters, and I want anyone curious about my setup to have the full picture in one place. For my broader thoughts on the ethics of using mods like Litematica and how I feel about all of this, you can check out my [[Ethical Statement|ethical statement here]]. The short version is that I think it comes down to personal preference and honesty about what you're doing, and I'm trying to hold myself to that standard.

## Mods
---
I run Fabric through Lunar Client. Most of what I use falls into quality of life and performance categories rather than gameplay changes. A handful of dependencies run in the background to support the listed mods and are not worth detailing individually.

**Axiom** — a powerful in-game building tool for creative mode work. This is how I design big builds before bringing them into survival.

**Armor Poser** — allows precise posing of armor stands, used on Hermitcraft and invaluable for decorative builds.

**Better Clouds** — improves cloud rendering for a more atmospheric look. Same setup Mumbo Jumbo uses, found it through him.

**Bundles Beyond** — shows the full contents of a bundle on hover rather than limiting the preview. A small but significant quality of life improvement, I don't really understand why vanilla doesn't have this.

**Chunky** — loads chunks in bulk to run Voxy smoothly before exploring an area.

**Concurrent Chunk Management Engine (C2ME)** — improves chunk loading performance and smoothness.

**Continuity** — enables connected textures, primarily used for glass.

**Flashback** — in-game recording tool for capturing footage without external software.

**Freecam** — essentially gives me a spectator mode camera that lets me set it in a fixed position while continuing to work on a build from another angle, essentially a tripod. Extremely helpful for building for specific angles/points of view.

**G4mespeed** — improves the reliability and feel of falling blocks, pistons, and elevators, which I initially got from Wildez after seeing him use his super fast elevator, which I've also implemented into my world.

**Invisible Frames** — makes item frames invisible when shift right-clicking them, useful for clean decorative setups.

**Litematica** — schematic tool primarily used for creative work. I am experimenting with using it for complex survival builds to better understand why so many builders rely on it. Check out my [[Ethical Statement|ethical statement]] for my full thoughts on this.

**Locator Heads** — shows other players' heads on the locator bar instead of a dot when friends are playing on the hosted world.

**MiniHUD** — allows, among many other things I don't use it for, toggling the display of light levels on blocks, making it much easier to efficiently light areas and catch any spawnable spots I missed.

**Mod Menu** — shows all installed mods in-game and allows quick access to their settings.

**Shade and Saturation Slider** — subtly tweaks shadows for a slightly shader-like feel without the performance cost of actual shaders. Originally from Mumbo Jumbo via Xisumavoid.

**Simple Fog Control** — lets me tune world fog to blend well with Voxy's LOD rendering.

**Voxy** — dramatically increases render distance using level of detail rendering, meaning distant terrain is displayed at lower detail the further away it is, the same technique most open world games use. This has completely elevated how much I enjoy looking around in Minecraft, it's fantastic.

## Shaders
---
I don't usually run shaders during normal gameplay due to performance. For recording and screenshots I use Photon shaders for their compatibility with Voxy. For general occasional use without Voxy I use Complementary shaders, which have been my preferred for quite a while now.

## Texture Packs
---
Everything I use comes from Vanilla Tweaks, split into separate downloads so I can toggle things on and off easily. Current setup includes:

**Connected Textures (Default)** — for Continuity. Mainly affects glass.

**Redstone and Utility tweaks** — kelp flowers showing max growth age, beehive honey levels filling gradually, subtle twinkling stars in the night sky, and smaller food and rocket icons.

**Visual experiments** — bushy leaves, smaller crosshair, 3D rails, ladders, vines, and bars, 3D bookshelves and chiseled bookshelves, quieter nether portals, and villager variants matching skin tone to biome. These are all things I am actively testing and may or may not keep.

## Data Packs
---
All data packs are from Vanilla Tweaks.

**Anti Enderman Grief** — prevents endermen from picking up and displacing blocks. For a long term world, the terrain damage they cause over time is purely destructive with no upside. I wish they only picked up naturally generated blocks and left player placed ones alone, but this is the next best thing.

**Multiplayer Sleep** — only one player needs to sleep to pass the night. Logical to me, and essential when friends are in different dimensions.

**Player Head Drops** — players drop their head on death. Fun, and potentially useful for builds.

**Silence Mobs** — reduces mob sound range. Primarily installed because happy ghasts are extremely loud and audible from a huge distance, which I find genuinely disruptive to the atmosphere of the world.

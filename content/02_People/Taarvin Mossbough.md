[[Taarvin Mossbough]] was a leader of the [[Thornkin]] of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]] in the period surrounding the [[Witch's Sundering]], and the principal drafter of the [[Pact of Root & Reed]]. He is among the most historically significant holders of the [[Mossbough]] name, remembered in both the Heartwood and [[Reedmarsh]] as the figure who formalized the alliance between the two communities at the moment it was most needed. He is also, though this is not how the Thornkin tend to frame it, the reason the Heartwood closed itself off from the wider world.

## Role
---
Taarvin assumed the Mossbough name during a period of significant instability in the southeastern [[The Great Swamp|Great Swamp]]. The Sundering had fractured the region's social fabric and left both the Heartwood and Reedmarsh exposed in ways that informal goodwill could no longer adequately address. His decision to approach Head Elder [[Fensar Reedhand]] of Reedmarsh and put the alliance in writing was a practical act as much as a symbolic one. The two communities had always been allies. Taarvin made sure that fact would survive whatever came next.

What is less formally recorded is what the Sundering cost him personally. [[Morwenna of the Hollow]] had been trained by the Thornkin, welcomed into the interior of the Heartwood, taught the pruning traditions that no outsider was meant to know. Taarvin had been part of that investment. The confrontation at the root table and everything that followed passed through his bond with [[Mother Tree|Vethara]] in ways he could not have anticipated or controlled. His grief and sense of betrayal flowed into the mother tree without his intending it, and she responded as she always responds to stimulus, completely and without hesitation. The thorned roots sealed the outer approaches. The full strength of the [[Verdant Veins]] pulled back toward the protected core. The outer zone of the Heartwood, the markets and the open paths and the world the Thornkin had built facing outward, died back over the generations that followed.

Taarvin did not command this. Whether he understood what had happened, or believed he had made a decision, is not recorded.

## Legacy
---
The [[Pact of Root & Reed]] is his most publicly acknowledged contribution. Within Thornkin tradition he is cited as a figure of foresight, one who understood that the bond between the Heartwood and Reedmarsh needed to be anchored in something more durable than mutual goodwill. His name surfaces wherever the Pact is invoked, which in the southeastern swamp is often. The other half of his legacy, the closed Heartwood, the moorland, the generations of slow withdrawal, sits in the oldest sections of the library and is not much discussed.

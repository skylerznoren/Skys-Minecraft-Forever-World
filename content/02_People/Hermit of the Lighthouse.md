The hermit is an old man of native and settler descent who lives in a small house beside [[The Lighthouse|the lighthouse]] on the stony peninsula above [[Main Trading Town|the trading town's]] northern harbor, where his father raised him. He tends the light and little else, rarely descending to the town below. ==His name is not yet established.==

## Background
---
His father was a native man from the village on the western mountain who married a settler woman and was rejected by his community for doing so. After his wife perished in ==a notable storm in the town's history==, his father attempted to return to the native village with his infant son and was turned away. He withdrew to the cliff above the harbor, built a small house there, and raised his son in it. Over the years that followed he constructed the lighthouse, so that what had taken his wife might not take others. He passed the lighthouse to his son when he could no longer tend it, and died not long after.

The hermit has lived on the peninsula his entire life, caught between the town below and the native village on the mountain without belonging fully to either, in the way that tends to happen to the children of people who crossed those kinds of lines.

## Character
---
He is quiet and self-sufficient, shaped by a life spent largely alone at the edge of things. His father's logbook and personal writings remain in the house on the peninsula. ==Whether the hermit has read them and what they contain is not yet established.==

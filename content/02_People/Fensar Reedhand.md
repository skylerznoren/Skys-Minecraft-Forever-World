[[Fensar Reedhand]] was the head elder of [[Reedmarsh]] in the period following the [[Witch's Sundering]] and one of the two signatories of the [[Pact of Root & Reed]], signed alongside [[Taarvin Mossbough]] of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]]. He is the tenth great-grandfather of the current head elder of Reedmarsh, and his decisions continue to shape the relationship between the two communities to this day.

## Role
---
Fensar led Reedmarsh during one of the most uncertain periods in the region's history. The Sundering had destabilized the social fabric of the southeastern [[The Great Swamp|Great Swamp]], and the communities that remained were navigating the aftermath without any formal framework to rely on. Accounts of him describe a pragmatic and steady figure, one who recognized that the informal alliance between Reedmarsh and the Heartwood needed to be put in writing before circumstances made it impossible to do so calmly.

## Legacy
---
Within Reedmarsh, Fensar is remembered as the elder who secured the village's future at a moment when the future was genuinely uncertain. The Reedhand name has carried weight in the community ever since, and the current head elder's lineage traces directly to him. His signature on the Pact of Root & Reed is the clearest expression of what he valued as a leader, the belief that stability is something that must be built deliberately rather than assumed.

[[Morwenna of the Hollow]] was a healer and seer of [[Reedmarsh]], once among the most respected practitioners of herbal craft and alchemical magic in the region. She is best known as the central figure of the [[Witch's Sundering]], the event that divided the swamp communities and produced the [[Exiled Witches]] as a distinct faction. She has not been seen in the southeastern swamp in many generations.

## Background
---
Morwenna was deeply attuned to the [[Verdant Veins]] from an early age, and her reputation for insight was such that it bordered on prophetic among those who knew her. She was a healer first, and her understanding of [[Mireblood]] and its alchemical properties was more developed than almost anyone else in the region. The [[Thornkin]] of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]] recognized this and brought her in to train under them, an honor extended to almost no one outside the Heartwood. She learned the pruning traditions that the Thornkin guard as closely as any secret they keep, and she spent time within the hollow of the mother tree itself as part of that training. It was the deepest access the Heartwood had ever granted an outsider.

It was this understanding that eventually put her at odds with the communities she belonged to. She had begun extracting Mireblood beyond what tradition permitted, using it to advance magical and alchemical work she believed to be genuinely beneficial. Her personal extractions were not large enough to threaten the swamp in any meaningful way, and she knew it. The elders of Reedmarsh knew it too, though they weren't all happy about it. What they could not accept was what her example had drawn toward her; practitioners with less careful intentions who had begun extracting in her name and with her arguments, and whose combined effect on the Verdant Veins was harder to dismiss.

Morwenna never fully accepted responsibility for what others had done in her wake. Whether this was pride, genuine conviction, or a refusal to abandon those who had aligned themselves with her is not something the historical record resolves cleanly.

## Appearance
---
Historical accounts describe her as tall and sharp-featured, with dark hair worn loose in a manner that marked her as unconventional within village custom, with a broad hat that covered her face when looking down. She favored deep green and violet, colors that would later become associated with the witches as a whole.

## The Hollow
---
The name she is remembered by accumulated across three distinct places and three chapters of her life, none of which were planned.

She is believed to have first worked out of the old witch hut to the west of Reedmarsh, a structure built on one of the most magically concentrated points in the region where the Verdant Veins run closest to the surface. As her work deepened and her relationship with the community grew complicated, she moved to a small hollow nestled into the cliff face near Reedmarsh, close enough to remain part of village life but separate from it in a way that suited her increasingly solitary practice. Among friends and family the nickname came naturally, affectionately at first, a gentle reference to the cave she clearly preferred to company.

The name became something more formal after her time training within the [[Mother Tree|mother tree's]] own hollow, the vast chamber inside the base of Vethara where the Thornkin keep their deepest knowledge. Having studied inside the hollow of the oldest living thing in the region, the name carried a different weight when she returned to Reedmarsh.

In her final years before the exile, she occupied the great cave beneath the northern plateau of the Thornspire Heartwood, where the small lake above drains silently into the rock below. At that scale the cave is less a hollow than a vast underground chamber, but it bears the same character; a space beneath something larger, tucked under the roots of the world. It was here that she carried out the deeper alchemical work that widened the rift between herself and the communities of the southeastern swamp, and it was from here that she eventually walked to the confrontation that ended everything.

It was the site of her original witch hut that the [[Sap Suckers]] later chose for the [[Cauldron Tap]], drawn to the same concentration of magical energy that Morwenna had worked from for years. Whether they knew its history is not recorded.

## The Exile and After
---
At the final confrontation in the Thornspire Heartwood, Morwenna broke a branch from the great root table at the base of the mother tree. She carried it north with her when she left, though what she intended with it, if anything, was not known to anyone who witnessed the moment.

She settled eventually in the northern reaches of the Great Swamp, in [[Morwenna's Abode|a place rumored to lie deep within the mangrove country far beyond the Heartwood's reach]], where the Verdant Veins dim and the land runs wild. What she has done there in the years since her exile is not known to anyone in Reedmarsh or the Heartwood. The northern swamp does not give up its information so easily.

The fear among the southeastern communities that she may one day return, leading her followers back with old grievances, has persisted for generations. There is no particular evidence to support it. There is also no evidence she is dead, which amounts to the same thing in the minds of those who grew up with her name as a warning.

## Legacy
---
Among the Swampfolk, Morwenna is remembered as the cautionary example of ambition untempered by restraint, the one whose hunger for power fractured something that had been whole. Among the witches who knew her, the account is different. What she actually believed, and what she became after walking north with a broken branch in her hand, belongs to the northern swamp now.

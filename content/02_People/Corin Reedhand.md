Corin Reedhand is the current head elder of [[Reedmarsh]] and a direct descendant of [[Fensar Reedhand]], who signed the [[Pact of Root & Reed]] generations ago. He is a measured and soft-spoken leader whose authority rests less on formal power than on the weight of his presence and the depth of his patience.

## Description
---
Corin carries a staff carved from [[Thornspire Heartwood|Heartwood]] timber, both a walking aid and a symbol of his stewardship. He listens more than he speaks, and those who mistake his restraint for passivity tend to revise that assessment. He came of age during the early years of the [[Cauldron Tap]]'s operation, and his understanding of conflict as something that erodes rather than erupts informs everything about how he leads.

## Role
---
As head elder, Corin presides over council matters, mediates disputes, and represents Reedmarsh in dealings with outside interests. He maintains cautious outward dialogue with the agents of the [[Sap Suckers]] while remaining privately uncompromising on the question of the swamp's protection. He consults [[Grandmother Eswa]] regularly, though this relationship is informal and largely unacknowledged in any official capacity.

## History
---
The Reedhand name has carried weight in Reedmarsh since Fensar's time, and Corin inherited both the lineage and the expectations that come with it. Whether his instinct for patience and slow resistance is purely strategic or shaped by something in his past is not something he has ever discussed openly.

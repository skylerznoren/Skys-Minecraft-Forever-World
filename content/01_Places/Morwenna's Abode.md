[[Morwenna's Abode]] is the dwelling of [[Morwenna of the Hollow]], located on a small island deep in the northern reaches of [[The Great Swamp|the Great Swamp]], within a mangrove swamp she chose specifically for its remoteness. The [[01_Places/Morwenna's Abode|Abode]] is unknown to the communities of [[Reedmarsh]] and the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], and most who remember her name assume she died long ago.

## Description
---
The island sits within a naturally occurring mangrove swamp in the wild northern reaches of the Great Swamp, just beyond the stabilizing influence of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]]'s roots. Morwenna retreated here after the [[Witch's Sundering]] to avoid being found, and the density of the mangrove growth immediately surrounding the island has increased considerably since her arrival. The cause is the young mangrove tree at the center of the island's clearing, grown from a branch of the great root table of the Thornspire Heartwood that Morwenna carried north with her on the day of her exile. Grafted onto a mangrove host and tended over centuries, it has begun to establish its own nascent network of [[Verdant Veins]] through its spreading roots, drawing the surrounding growth inward and thickening the mangrove canopy around the island in a way that makes approach difficult without appearing to do so deliberately.

The Abode itself stands in the clearing alongside the tree, which has grown into the structure over the long years of its cultivation. Both are works of patience rather than haste.

## Significance
---
The northern Verdant Veins are thin and unregulated compared to those of the southeastern swamp, weakened by their youth and distance from the Thornspire. The young tree at the center of the Abode's clearing is the only cutting of the original Thornspire lineage that exists outside the Heartwood, and the nascent Verdant Veins it is producing represent the first new anchor point the northern swamp has ever had. Whether the communities of the southeastern swamp would consider this an act of preservation or a continuation of everything they exiled Morwenna for is an open question, and one that cannot be asked without revealing that she is still alive.

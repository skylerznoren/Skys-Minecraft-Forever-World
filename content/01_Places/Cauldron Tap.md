The [[Cauldron Tap]] is an industrial extraction structure built atop the site of an old witch hut on the western edge of [[Reedmarsh]], operated by the [[Sap Suckers]] for the purpose of harvesting [[Mireblood]] from [[The Great Swamp|the Great Swamp]]. It is the most visible and immediate threat to the stability of the [[Verdant Veins]] in the region.

## Description
---
The structure is unlike anything else in the swamp. Its body is predominantly copper, oxidized to a pale green that makes it difficult to distinguish from the surrounding vegetation at a distance -- whether by accident or design is unclear. A large containment vat sits at the top, into which raw Mireblood is drawn up from the peat below. From there the liquid is processed internally, its component materials separated and expelled through a series of output channels that extend from the lower body of the structure like legs anchoring it into the ground, each dedicated to a different refined yield.

The overall shape is vaguely organic and deeply unsettling, something between a machine and a creature with its roots driven into the earth. The swampfolk do not look at it longer than they have to.

Not all of the Mireblood drawn up is successfully processed. A residual seepage drains from the base of the structure back into the surrounding peat, carrying post-processed runoff into the swamp's water table. What effect this corrupted material has on the Verdant Veins over time is not yet fully understood, but the land immediately surrounding the Tap has changed noticeably since its installation.

## History
---
The Tap was installed within living memory of the oldest inhabitants of Reedmarsh. Its arrival was not announced. By the time the swampfolk understood what was being built and what it was doing, it was already operational. The old witch hut it was built upon had carried its own weight of story and cautionary memory among the local people, and its repurposing was felt as something beyond a practical grievance.

The site was chosen deliberately. Witch huts are not built at random -- they are placed where the Verdant Veins run close to the surface, at intersections of magical current where the land's energy is most concentrated and accessible. Whoever designed the Cauldron Tap understood this.

The [[Thornspire Heartwood]] is an ancient forest set atop a plateau rising from [[The Great Swamp|the Great Swamp]], home to the [[Thornkin]] and the oldest living thing in the region, the [[Mother Tree|mother tree]] they call [[Mother Tree|Vethara]] in ritual and blessing. It is the anchor point of the [[Verdant Veins]] and the most defensible and secretive settlement in the southeastern swamp.

## Description
---
The Heartwood stands apart from the swamp in every sense. Where the swamp is open and sparse, the Heartwood is dense and towering, its canopy closing out the sky above a forest floor thick with roots, ferns, and undergrowth. The plateau it sits on is largely flat across its western and eastern reaches, with a small natural lake on the northern edge that drains silently into a cave beneath the rock. The southern face drops in a sheer cliff directly above [[Reedmarsh]] and the swamp below, making the Heartwood visible from almost anywhere in the surrounding wetlands.

The forest is not a natural old growth wood in any ordinary sense. The trees that grow throughout the Heartwood are extensions of the mother tree herself, connected to her through the root network and sustained by the full strength of the Verdant Veins flowing beneath the plateau. None of these trees grow anywhere outside the Heartwood. Separated from the mother tree's root network they wither and die, a fact the Thornkin understand well and outsiders do not.

The canopy drips constantly, the moisture drawn up from the surrounding swamp through the mother tree's vast root system releasing back down through the leaves in a perpetual fine rain that keeps the forest floor damp and dim regardless of the weather above.

## History
---
The Heartwood was not always as closed as it is now. In an earlier period, its outer reaches were open to traders, visitors, and travelers from across the region, a bustling outer zone where the Thornkin engaged with the wider world while keeping the interior restricted. The [[Witch's Sundering]] ended this. [[Taarvin Mossbough]], feeling the full weight of [[Morwenna of the Hollow]]'s betrayal through his bond with the mother tree, passed that grief and hurt into Vethara without intending to. She responded the only way she could, pulling inward, thickening her defenses, redirecting the full strength of the Verdant Veins away from the outer reaches and back toward the protected core, dimming the surrounding swamp to its natural character rather than extinguishing the Veins entirely. The extension trees of the outer zone, cut off from the full strength of the root network, died back over the following generations, leaving the moorland that now rings the dead outer boundary of what the Heartwood once was.

The Thornkin do not discuss the moorland. The records of the outer zone's existence sit in the oldest sections of the library inside the mother tree's base, and those sections are rarely opened.

## The Moorland
---
Beyond the living tree line, where the Heartwood's outer boundary once stood, lies a stretch of dead and moor-like ground where the old extension trees have long since withered. The ruins of what the Thornkin built during their more expansive period stand here, aqueducts, market halls, and fortress walls, now cracked and overgrown with thorned scrub and pale moss. The thorned roots that sealed the outer approaches during the Sundering still push through this ground, gnarled and dead at their tips but no less sharp.

The Thornkin treat this area as a place to be avoided rather than a place that holds meaning, drawing spiritual unease subconsciously from the evidence of their homeland's decline.

## Defenses
---
The Heartwood's only open approach is from the south, through Reedmarsh, formalized through the [[Pact of Root & Reed]]. Every other route is blocked by cliff faces, thorned root growth, or the dead ground of the moorland beyond the living tree line. The Thornkin know the terrain of the plateau intimately and are not reluctant to use that knowledge. The thorned growth along the accessible edges is both natural and cultivated, maintained through pruning traditions passed down within the Thornkin and known to no one outside the Heartwood, with one historical exception.

## Present
---
The [[Cauldron Tap]] to the west has been draining the Verdant Veins steadily since its installation within living memory of Reedmarsh's eldest inhabitants. The Heartwood feels this. The Verdant Veins remain active and connected to Vethara throughout the swamp, as evidenced by the Cauldron Tap's continued operation, but their flow is being drawn upon in ways the land was never meant to sustain. The mother tree's canopy drips a little less than it once did. The extension trees at the inner edges of the plateau grow more slowly. The current [[Mossbough]], bonded to Vethara and therefore sensitive to everything she absorbs from the root network, has been declining in health in ways the Thornkin attribute to age. The true cause has not been identified, and the Mossbough has not yet connected their own deterioration to the disturbance in the west.

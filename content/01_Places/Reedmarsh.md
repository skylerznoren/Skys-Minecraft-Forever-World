[[Reedmarsh]] is a village built at the southern foot of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], where the [[The Great Swamp|Great Swamp]] meets the old growth forest rising on the hill above. Its outer reaches are classic swamp settlement, stilt houses connected by plank walkways above waterlogged ground, but its heart sits higher on a natural hill whose slopes are carved through by marshy ravines and gorges that channel all foot traffic toward the village center. The great tree of the Heartwood looms to the northwest, visible from anywhere in the surrounding swamp despite sitting a good distance away.

## Description
---
The village occupies two distinct elevations. The lower reaches spread out into the swamp proper, where structures are raised on timber pilings above the water and connected by rope-bound bridges worn smooth by generations of use. The upper settlement clusters around the hill, where ravines cutting through the terrain create a landscape of natural bridges and enclosed pathways that give the village an almost maze-like approach from the south. The swamp itself is open and sparse, dotted with gnarled oak and broken by stretches of still dark water.

To the north the hill climbs directly into the Thornspire Heartwood, whose southern face drops in a sheer cliff above the swamp floor. There is no way into the Heartwood from the south without passing through Reedmarsh first.

## Culture
---
The people of Reedmarsh live in practiced harmony with the swamp. Nothing is taken without acknowledgment, and extraction is always measured against what the land can sustain. Knowledge of the [[Verdant Veins]] is common among all who live in and around the swamp, understood as simply the nature of the place rather than any specialized or privileged understanding. The Swampfolk of Reedmarsh are wary of outsiders by habit rather than hostility, and their position as the sole passage into the Heartwood from the south is not something they take lightly.

## Function
---
Reedmarsh maintains trade relations with several communities, not all of which are well documented. The village also sits closest to the [[Cauldron Tap]], the outside operation extracting [[Mireblood]] from the swamp to the west. Its eldest inhabitants remember the swamp before the Tap was installed, and the difference is not lost on them.

## History
---
The village's relationship with the Heartwood community is formalized through the [[Pact of Root & Reed]], struck generations ago between Reedmarsh and the people of the Thornspire Heartwood. The agreement reflects a long history of mutual dependence between the two settlements, one that the geography of the place makes self-evident.

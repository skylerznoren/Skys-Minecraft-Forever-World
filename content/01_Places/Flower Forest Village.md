The [[Flower Forest Village]] is a settlement on the [[Flower Forest Peninsula|flower forest peninsula]], shaped by generations of [[Apiarianism|Apiarian]] tradition. It is best known across the region for its honey, which is traded widely and considered among the finest available.

## Description
---
Homes are built with integrated gardens and carefully tended hives, their flower beds spilling from one property into the next beneath the shade of great oaks. Shrines tucked between oak roots receive small offerings of pollen, wax, and honey — quiet acts of devotion woven into daily life.

## Culture
---
Village life follows the rhythm of bloom cycles and harvests. Bees are treated as companions rather than livestock, and children are taught from a young age how to conduct themselves around a hive. Festivals mark the turning of each season.

## Function
---
The village produces and exports honey. [[House Ambrose]] operates the largest apiary in the village and serves as the primary broker between local producers and outside traders, consolidating much of the village's output for bulk sale.

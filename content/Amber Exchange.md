The Amber Exchange is a factoring house situated at the crest of the hill in the [[Flower Forest Village]], operated by [[House Ambrose]] as the central point of trade for the village's honey output. It is the face of the Ambrose commercial operation and the place where outside traders come to do business.

## Description
---
The building sits at a prominent central position slightly above the rest of the village, accessible by cart from the main path. A commercial front handles negotiations and transactions, while the side and interior of the building provides storage for honey brought in by villagers, kept in barrels awaiting collection. Carts loaded with stock pull away from the Exchange regularly, carrying purchases out to wider distribution networks.

## Function
---
Villagers who choose to sell their honey through the Ambrose name bring it to the Exchange, where it is received, graded, and stored until sold. [[Lucien Ambrose]] oversees all commercial dealings from here, negotiating prices with traders and brokers on the village's behalf. The Ambrose family takes a cut of each sale in exchange for their role as broker.

The family's own honey is stored separately from the collective stock, distinguished by its deep amber color and singular flavor profile, and commands a considerably higher price.

The Natural Observatory and Void Authority, known by its initials as NOVA, is a geological survey concern operating in and around [[Main Trading Town|the trading town]] and its surrounding cave systems. It holds a longstanding contract with the town's administrative authority to monitor cave stability, map underground formations, and assess geological conditions in the cliffside and subterranean environment the town is built upon. Its restricted access notices are a familiar presence in the caves above the harbor, and its reports have been delivered to the trade yard administration on a regular schedule for as long as anyone currently working there can remember.

## Origins
---
NOVA came to the town's attention following a tunnel collapse on the path east of the [[Trade Yard|trade yard]] near the southeastern waterfall, an incident that threatened a route the town depended on. A geological survey concern was recommended to the town through an anonymous intermediary, offered its services at no cost, and resolved the situation competently enough that the town formalized the arrangement shortly after. NOVA was granted a standing government contract and a degree of autonomous authority over the cave systems, with the mayor holding nominal oversight in their capacity as chief civic authority. ==The precise origins of NOVA and the circumstances surrounding the tunnel collapse have not been examined in any surviving record.==

## Function
---
NOVA employs fewer than ten surface scientists whose work keeps them in the cave systems most of the time, checking stability, updating maps, and replacing signage where conditions warrant. Certain sections of the cave network are marked as restricted entirely, their entrances bearing NOVA notices citing geological hazard or active survey work. These closures are treated as routine by anyone who encounters them. The organization operates out of a small office in the town hall, where its reports are filed and its administrative correspondence handled.

Its management structure is not publicly documented. Directives to the office arrive through internal channels and are followed by the surface staff without particular scrutiny, as is common in organizations where the work is practical and the administration remote. NOVA holds autonomous hiring authority within its own operation, meaning its staffing decisions require no government approval. Its contract with the trade yard administration has not been formally reviewed in living memory, and the payments processed against it appear as standard civic survey expenditure in the administrative records.

## Presence in the Cave Systems
---
NOVA's most visible presence in the town is its signage. Restricted access notices appear throughout the cave network surrounding the trading town, citing geological instability or active survey operations. Most residents have encountered one at some point and given it no further thought. The caves beyond those notices are not discussed by NOVA's surface staff, who understand them to fall outside their designated survey routes and clearance.

The full nature and purpose of NOVA is documented under [[The Continuance]].

[[House Ambrose]] is the most prominent family in the [[Flower Forest Village]], renowned for their apiary and their central role in the village's honey trade. For generations the Ambrose name has been synonymous with [[Apiarianism|Apiarian]] devotion, and they are widely regarded as living proof that harmony with the bees brings prosperity. The name Ambrose is old, its origins traced by scholars to a word meaning divine, a detail the family has never discouraged.

## Structure
---
The household is led by **Melisande** (honey, immortal) Ambrose, whose word is final in matters of family and trade. Her husband **Edric** (prosperous ruler) shares governance of the estate as a close equal. Together they form the innermost circle of the family, and between them they hold its deepest secret.

Their eldest son **Lucien** (light) is considerably older than his siblings, and has long since taken on the family's commercial dealings. He is stationed at the [[Ambrose Honey Exchange]], the factoring house at the crest of the village hill, where he negotiates with traders and brokers on behalf of both the family and the villagers who sell their honey through the Ambrose name. Their eldest daughter **Anthea** (blossom) is well regarded in the village and carries the family's reputation with quiet grace, untouched by the burdens her brother bears. The younger members of the household, **Rowan** and **Isolde**, remain uninvolved in the family's operations, benefiting from the Ambrose name without knowing the cost of it.

## Function
---
House Ambrose operates the largest apiary in the village and acts as the primary broker for the village's collective honey output. Villagers who choose to sell through the family bring their stock to the [[Ambrose Honey Exchange]], where it is stored, graded, and prepared for transport. The family's own honey is kept deliberately separate, distinguished by its deep amber color and a richness of flavor that lingers long after tasting, and commands a considerably higher price from traders who seek it out.

## Reputation
---
Within the village, the Ambroses are treated as exemplars of Apiarian life. Their honey is considered the finest available, its unusual consistency and richness quietly attributed to divine favor. Few think to question what else might explain it. Beyond the village, traders carry word of Ambrose honey to distant markets, some claiming its quality carries something ineffable, a brightness and depth that no other producer can replicate.

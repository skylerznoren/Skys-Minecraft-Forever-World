The [[Thornkin]] are the people of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], a druidic culture that has inhabited the ancient spruce forest and guarded the mother tree at its center for as long as any record in the region reaches. They are distinct from the [[Swampfolk]] of the surrounding wetlands, and the two peoples, while long allied through the [[Pact of Root & Reed]], are not the same.

## Description
---
The Thornkin rarely leave the Heartwood. Their lives are organized entirely around the forest and the mother tree at its center, known in ritual and blessing by her sacred name [[Mother Tree|Vethara]], whose roots spreading outward through the plateau and into the swamp define both the physical and spiritual boundaries of their world. In daily life she is referred to simply as the mother tree. They are deeply attuned to the [[Verdant Veins]] and understand the health of the mother tree and the health of the swamp as inseparable. The thorn defenses that line the accessible edges of the Heartwood plateau are both natural growth and deliberate cultivation, a practice the Thornkin have learned from and emulated across generations.

## Governance
---
The Thornkin are led by whoever is judged most fit for the role, chosen without regard to family or bloodline. Upon taking leadership, that person sets aside their birth name and assumes the name Mossbough, belonging thereafter to the Heartwood rather than to any family within it. The bond is not purely ceremonial. The current [[Mossbough]] is understood to be connected to the mother tree itself in their role as its leading guardian, a connection that makes the health of the tree and the health of the leader difficult to separate. As the [[Cauldron Tap]] continues to drain the [[Mireblood]] and damage the [[Verdant Veins]] to the west, the current Mossbough has been declining in ways the Thornkin have attributed to age. The true cause has not yet been fully identified.

## Values
---
The Thornkin hold the mother tree and the Verdant Veins as sacred above all else. Their guardianship is not passive, as they are not reluctant to defend the Heartwood by force, and their knowledge of the terrain and its natural defenses makes the plateau a difficult place to approach without permission. Outsiders are rarely welcomed inside the forest, and those who enter without invitation do not always find their way back out without assistance.

Their alliance with Reedmarsh is their most significant external relationship, one they take seriously. Beyond that, they engage with the wider world sparingly and on their own terms.

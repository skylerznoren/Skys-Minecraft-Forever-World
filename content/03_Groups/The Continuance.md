The Continuance is the name the workers of Project NEXUS gave themselves in the aftermath of [[Avon Brownigan|Avon Brownigan's]] death, when word reached the facility that operations would not change but would continue in his absence. Technically employees of the [[Natural Observatory and Void Authority|Natural Observatory and Void Authority (NOVA)]], they had always understood themselves through the work rather than the employer, and when the work became the only thing left of the man who had defined it, the Continuance was the name that filled the space he had left behind.

## Origins
---
Avon established the operation immediately after his visit to the vault, structuring it so that no single person beneath him held the full picture. To give the operation civic cover while keeping it at legal distance from [[Brownigan & Sons]], he first needed a credible institution behind it. A tunnel collapse on the path east of the trade yard near the southeastern waterfall provided the opportunity. ==Whether the collapse was natural or engineered has never been formally examined.== Avon recommended the services of a geological survey concern he had apparently worked with during the kelp farm excavation, keeping his own name out of the arrangement entirely. That concern, NOVA, assessed and mitigated the damage at no cost to the town. Avon's quiet advocacy helped secure them a formal government contract with autonomous hiring authority and standing civic status shortly after. The town got a useful institution. Avon got a puppet with a government seal.

With NOVA established, he structured Project NEXUS beneath it so that NOVA provided real geological reports, employed real scientists who knew nothing of what lay beneath their work, and maintained enough civic credibility that its contract with the trade yard administration was never seriously examined. Project NEXUS documents generated within the facility referenced only NOVA as their operating authority. Brownigan & Sons' books showed only routine payments to NOVA for survey services. No document connected all three in the same place.

Avon also designed a succession system for each functional role within the facility, sealed dossiers held internally and handed to whoever arrived to fill a given position by a dedicated intake worker, containing only what that role needed to know. The system was built to outlast him without requiring anyone to hold the complete picture.

## Recruitment
---
Facility workers are recruited through a broker network operating in distant port towns, a standing arrangement Avon established and that has renewed itself through written instruction ever since. Brokers advertise the work locally as well paying long term employment with housing and meals included, vague on specifics but attractive to men with limited prospects. NOVA pays the brokers a flat finder's fee per placement. The workers pay nothing upfront, the debt being structured into their wage arrangement once inside the facility, calibrated carefully enough that the balance never quite reaches zero.

Workers arrive in the trading town as ordinary travelers, pass through the NOVA office in the [[Main Trading Town|town hall]] for onboarding documentation, and are directed by map to a concealed entrance east of the crane area in a waterfall cave in the cliffside. The outer door bears NOVA hazard signage and opens by the map credential itself. Beyond it a short passage leads to a checkpoint where a facility worker behind a partition verifies papers before opening the inner door. Workers surrender their documents at this point. They do not return to the surface. The facility provides dormitories, and dried kelp piped down from the factory above supplies the bulk of their food.

## Structure
---
At its operational peak the facility housed a rotating workforce divided across research, extraction, maintenance, and distribution. Research documented the vault's byproduct gas and the crystalline formations condensing in the ventilation shaft. Extraction maintained the arm and tube infrastructure drawing shells from the Nexus. Maintenance kept the facility's machinery running. Distribution moved shells through a concealed entrance connecting the facility to the storage cave behind the [[The Trade Yard|trade yard]], obscuring them among legitimate cargo for collection by an overseas broker.

In the present day the workforce is at its lowest since the operation began. Decades of incremental automation have reduced the number of active roles considerably, and the facility has not been recruiting at the rate needed to replace those who have been lost to time, deterioration, or the effects of prolonged vault proximity. Certain sections of the facility have fallen into disrepair. Equipment that breaks down stays broken if nobody currently working there understands it well enough to fix it. ==The precise current headcount and state of the facility's various sections are still being established.==

## The Drift
---
Avon's succession system degraded with each passing generation, the sealed dossiers recopied and reinterpreted by people who understood less of the original design than those before them. His recorded instructions became founding documents. His decisions became doctrine. The nested squares symbol of the vault's geometry, adopted as the Project NEXUS emblem, acquired the quality of a seal and then of something closer to a sigil. Members of the current Continuance treat it with a reverence they would struggle to articulate. They do not know it is the geometry of the vault's face seen from a specific angle. They know it is theirs, and that it was his, and that is sufficient.

## Current State
---
The Continuance functions because its compartmentalized structure makes function self-sustaining, each remaining worker doing what the role has always done without needing to understand the whole. The research division continues documenting phenomena it cannot explain. The gap between their accumulated records and their actual comprehension of the vault has widened steadily across the organization's history.

The cavern fan has broken down and gone unreplaced. Trace amounts of byproduct gas have been permeating the lower cavern, and those spending the most time there have been affected in ways the organization has noted without addressing. The drift toward erratic behavior and intensified devotion that has characterized the Continuance in recent years may owe something to this. No one inside has connected those two facts.

[[Bramwell Trembly|Bram Brownigan]] works above them without knowledge of their existence. The Continuance is aware of him and has reached no consensus about what his presence means.

The [[Exiled Witches]] are the descendants and successors of those who were driven from the communities of [[Reedmarsh]] and the [[Thornspire Heartwood]] during the [[Witch's Sundering]]. Before the Sundering they were not a distinct faction at all, but ordinary members of the swamp communities; healers, herbalists, and practitioners of subtle magic who lived and worked alongside fishermen, farmers, and elders without meaningful separation. The exile made them into something else.

## Origins
---
The people who became the Exiled Witches did not choose that identity. They chose [[Morwenna of the Hollow]] when the community forced them to pick a side, or they were swept into the conflict by proximity and association. Some believed in her arguments. Others simply could not bring themselves to condemn someone they had known their whole lives. When Morwenna walked north, they followed.

What they found on the other side of that walk was not a new community but a scattered dispersal. Morwenna herself withdrew entirely, leaving those who had followed her without the figurehead they had sacrificed everything for. Whatever unity the exiled group had came apart without her, and the generations that followed drifted further from each other and from the world they had left behind.

## What They Became
---
Cut off from community and operating in increasingly isolated circumstances, the descendants of the exiled witches drifted toward the dangerous and unpredictable behavior the Swampfolk had originally feared. The irony is not lost on those in Reedmarsh who think carefully about the history, as the exile may have produced the very thing it was meant to prevent. Witches encountered in the wilder reaches of the swamp and beyond are rarely the measured practitioners of herbal craft their ancestors were. Generations of isolation have a cost.

Not all followed the same path. Some integrated quietly into other communities under assumed identities. Others joined the pillagers. A few live alone in their huts. There is no unified body, no shared leadership, and no known communication between whatever remnants of the original exile still persist.

## Those Who Stayed
---
Not everyone with ties to magical practice left with Morwenna. Some stayed in Reedmarsh, recanting or simply weathering the conflict without being drawn into the final confrontation. Their descendants remain in the community today, carrying a complicated and largely unspoken relationship with the history. The line between a Swampfolk elder with deep knowledge of Mireblood and a witch is not always as clear as the Sundering's legacy would suggest.

## Relationship to the Present
---
[[Swampfolk of Reedmarsh]] maintain an official position of exile that has never been formally revisited. In practice, attitudes vary. Older residents carry the Sundering as a lived inheritance and tend toward wariness. Younger generations are more likely to question whether the exile was proportionate, or necessary, or even fully honest in how it has been remembered. The arrival of the [[Cauldron Tap]] and the behavior of the [[Sap Suckers]] has sharpened this questioning for some; the thing the witches were exiled for pales against what outsiders are now doing openly and without consequence.

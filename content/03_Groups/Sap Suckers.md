The Sap Suckers is the name used by [[Swampfolk of Reedmarsh|the Swampfolk of Reedmarsh]] and others in the region for the outside faction operating the [[Cauldron Tap]] and extracting [[Mireblood]] from [[The Great Swamp|the Great Swamp]] for outside sale. Their true identity, origin, and organizational backing remain unknown to anyone in the swamp.

## Operations
---
Extracted Mireblood, known in outside markets as [[Mireblood|Witchsap]], is processed through the Cauldron Tap into its various refined yields before being loaded into unmarked barrels for transport. On the road they present as ordinary traders, moving their cargo through normal commercial channels without drawing attention to its origin or nature. Whether they also sell quantities of raw unprocessed Mireblood through separate channels is not known to anyone in the swamp.

## Organization
---
The industrial character of the Cauldron Tap points toward resources, expertise, and planning beyond what opportunistic outside operators could reasonably provide on their own. The Sap Suckers may be hired hands working for a larger organization or patron, but the nature of that backing, if it exists, has never surfaced. They have never offered an account of themselves to anyone in the region.

## Presence in the Swamp
---
The Sap Suckers maintain a presence at and around the Cauldron Tap sufficient to keep it operational but are otherwise not visible within the swamp. The Swampfolk are aware of them and have begun interfering with their work in small ways, disrupting access and spoiling shipments where possible. The Sap Suckers have not visibly responded to this, whether out of indifference, patience, or the confidence of something with larger backing is an open question.

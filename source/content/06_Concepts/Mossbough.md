[[Mossbough]] is the name and title assumed by the leader of the [[Thornkin]] upon taking their position as guardian of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]] and the [[Mother Tree]]. It is not inherited by blood or passed through family lines, but given to whoever the Thornkin judge most fit to bear it. Upon assuming the name, the new Mossbough sets aside their family name and replaces it with Mossbough, keeping only their given name. They belong to the Heartwood from that point forward rather than to any family within it.

## The Name
---
Every leader of the Thornkin throughout recorded history has carried Mossbough as their family name, distinguished from one another only by the given name they kept upon taking the title. [[Taarvin Mossbough]], who signed the [[Pact of Root & Reed]] alongside Head Elder [[Fensar Reedhand]] of [[Reedmarsh]] in the aftermath of the [[Witch's Sundering]], is among the most historically significant holders of the title. The current Mossbough continues a lineage of stewardship that predates written record.

## The Bond
---
The role of Mossbough carries more than administrative responsibility. The leader of the Thornkin serves as the primary sensory interface between [[Mother Tree|Vethara]] and the world beyond what roots and weather can reach. Vethara is not conscious in the way a person is conscious. She does not deliberate or decide. But she is responsive, receiving stimuli through the root network and through the Verdant Veins in ways that have shaped the Heartwood for longer than memory holds. The Mossbough extends that sensitivity. Through the bond, Vethara receives what she otherwise could not, the emotional and experiential world of the person connected to her, and she responds to it the way a plant responds to any stimulus, not with intention, but with growth, with reaction, with the blind intelligence of something immeasurably old.

When the tree is stable and the [[Verdant Veins]] run strong, the Mossbough tends to be sharp and vigorous regardless of age. When the tree is under strain, the Mossbough feels it in ways that are not always easy to distinguish from ordinary decline. The reverse is also true. When [[Taarvin Mossbough]] felt the full weight of [[Morwenna of the Hollow]]'s betrayal during the Witch's Sundering, that grief passed into Vethara through the bond without him intending it to. She responded by pulling inward, sealing the outer Heartwood with thorned root growth and redirecting the Verdant Veins toward the protected core. Taarvin did not command this. The tree felt his pain and responded to it as she responds to all things, as nature does, completely and without hesitation.

This is considered both the weight and the honor of the position. No Mossbough has ever renounced the name once taken.

## Selection
---
The process by which a new Mossbough is chosen is an internal matter of the Thornkin and not well documented outside the Heartwood. It is known that the selection is made collectively and that fitness for the role is weighted above all other considerations. What fitness means in practice, and who judges it, is not something the Thornkin have shared with outside communities. Given the nature of the bond, it is reasonable to assume that whoever is chosen must be capable of bearing what Vethara gives back through it, which is not nothing.

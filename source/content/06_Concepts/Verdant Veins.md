## Overview

The Verdant Veins are invisible yet palpable energy currents running beneath the swamp and forest alike, binding water, root, and stone in a living web.

They form the underlying magical structure of the region.

***
## Cultural Interpretation

The Swampfolk regard the Verdant Veins as a sacred trust, something to be tended rather than controlled.

The witches once sought to manipulate these currents through brewing and ritual amplification.

Modern outsiders, whether unknowingly or uncaringly, disrupt the flow through industrial extraction driven by the Cauldron Tap.

[[Grandmother Eswa]] is an elder of [[Reedmarsh]] and among the most respected living members of the swamp community. She holds no formal title, but the current head elder and others regularly seek her counsel before making consequential decisions. She is one of the few people still alive who remembers the [[Cauldron Tap|Cauldron Tap's]] installation, and what the swamp felt like before it.

## Background
---
Eswa's family remained in Reedmarsh after the [[Witch's Sundering]], renouncing any affiliation with those who followed [[Morwenna of the Hollow]] and keeping their heads down through the years that followed. The practice of magic became taboo in the community after the exile, and her ancestors carried their knowledge quietly, passing it down within the family without drawing attention to its origins. Eswa knows what she comes from. She does not advertise it.

Her understanding of [[Mireblood]], the [[Verdant Veins]], and the older herbal traditions of the swamp is deeper than almost anyone in Reedmarsh would guess, and deeper than she lets on to most. She practices restraint not only as a philosophy but as a survival instinct inherited across generations. The taboo that drove her ancestors to silence has never fully lifted, even now.

## Role
---
Eswa does not lead through authority. She leads through the weight of what she knows and how long she has known it. People come to her with problems they do not know how to name, and she has a way of naming them. The head elder of Reedmarsh is among those who do this regularly, though the dynamic between them is not formally acknowledged.

She is openly distrustful of the Cauldron Tap and does not conceal this. Among the older residents of Reedmarsh she is not alone in that distrust, but she carries it with more context than most. She remembers the swamp before the Tap and understands what is being lost in terms that go beyond the practical.

## Appearance
---
Bent with age but sharp-eyed, Eswa wears deep green and muted purple, colors old enough in swamp tradition that most in the village would not think to read anything into them. Her silver hair is braided with swamp reeds. She looks fragile to those who do not know her.

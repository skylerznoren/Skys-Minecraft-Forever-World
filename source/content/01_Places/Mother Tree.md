The [[Mother Tree]] is the ancient tree at the heart of the [[Thornspire Heartwood]], known to the [[Thornkin]] by her sacred name [[Mother Tree|Vethara]], spoken only in ritual and blessing. She is the sole known example of the thornspire, a species unlike any other in the region, and the anchor point of the [[Verdant Veins]] that run through the entire southeastern [[The Great Swamp|Great Swamp]].

The name Vethara derives from *vetus*, a Latin root meaning old or long-established, and *ara*, appearing in Latin as a word for sacred hearth or altar. Together the name carries the sense of an ancient sacred center.

## Description
---
The Mother Tree stands at the highest point of the Heartwood plateau, toward its western edge, rising above the surrounding forest in a way that makes her visible from the swamp below on clear days. Her canopy spreads wide and irregularly, unlike the upward growth of the spruce around her. Her trunk and roots are brown in the way that old things are brown, dark and varied in tone, with roots that push up through the plateau soil in great arching formations before plunging back into the earth, creating natural barriers and enclosed spaces at her base. Thorned growth emerges at intervals along her exposed roots and lower branches. She does not look like anything else in the forest.

Her roots do not stay within the Heartwood. They extend downward through the cliff faces of the plateau into the swamp below, spreading outward through the peat and water in a network that underpins the Verdant Veins of the southeastern swamp. The great root table at her base, where the [[Thornkin]] have long gathered for matters of importance, is formed from the convergence of her largest surface roots.

## Significance
---
To the Thornkin, the Mother Tree is not merely old or large. She is the reason the swamp is what it is. The Verdant Veins flow because she anchors them. The [[Mireblood]] that accumulates in the deep peat of the southeastern swamp is a product of the magical currents she sustains. Every community that draws on the swamp's resources, whether they know it or not, depends on her continued health.

The bond between the Mother Tree and the current [[Mossbough]] is understood by the Thornkin as the most direct expression of their guardianship. The leader of the Thornkin does not merely protect the tree from outside harm. They are connected to her in a way that makes their wellbeing inseparable from hers. As the [[Cauldron Tap]] drains the Verdant Veins to the west, this connection has become a source of quiet alarm among those Thornkin who have begun to notice their leader's decline.

## History
---
No record exists of the Mother Tree being planted or grown. She has simply always been there, older than the Thornkin's own history in the Heartwood. As the only known thornspire in existence, her loss would be without remedy, though one cutting of her lineage exists outside the forest, carried north by [[Morwenna of the Hollow]] on the day of the [[Witch's Sundering]]. No one but Morwenna is aware of this.

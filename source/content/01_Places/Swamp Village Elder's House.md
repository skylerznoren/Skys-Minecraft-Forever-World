## Overview

The Swamp Village Elder’s House is a low, broad-shouldered stilt home situated at the center of Reedmarsh. Built higher than most surrounding structures, it rests on thick timber supports driven deep into peat and root.

Reed mats line the interior walls, carved charms hang from beams, and a central hearth burns steadily as both warmth and symbol of continuity.

***
## The Attic Archives

The attic holds scrolls, carved tablets, and preserved relics documenting generations of swamp history. Records of the Witch’s Sundering, early trade agreements, and copies of foundational pacts are stored in sealed cases to protect them from damp and decay.

Access to these materials is traditionally restricted to the Elder and trusted lorekeepers.

***
# Cultural Significance

Beyond being a residence, the house functions as a quiet seat of memory. Disputes are settled here, seasonal rites are announced here, and history is guarded here.

It stands not as a symbol of power, but of stewardship.
